## The image

A 6-panel grid (1024x683 PNG) with a "23 NOVEMBER 2026 — THE WORLD IN
ONE DAY" banner. The six stories, all events that happened, were first
reported, or dominated the news cycle on 23 November 2026:

1. **COP31 collapses** — the Antalya climate summit ends with no deal
   after record overtime; the EU and about 130 parties reject a text
   that omits fossil fuels. The collapse dominated Monday's front pages.
2. **Nikkei -5%** — Asian stocks slide on Monday as a sell-off in US AI
   shares spreads; Japan's Nikkei drops 5.06%.
3. **Democrats 258 seats** — Democrats clinch at least 258 US House
   seats, their largest majority since 1992, after a North Carolina
   recount win.
4. **Seoul minister quits** — South Korea's education minister resigns
   over the AI-assisted exam-cheating scandal.
5. **Hormuz tankers hit** — tanker attacks surge in the Strait of
   Hormuz; seven tankers hit since 16 November, and a search is under
   way for a missing sailor.
6. **UK to host 2035 World Cup** — FIFA is set to confirm the UK as host
   of the 2035 Women's World Cup, the first World Cup in the UK since
   1966.

No recognizable faces of living people appear. No graphic violence.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only) copies the pre-built `entry.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`) and verifies it is a PNG under
1 MiB. No network, no randomness — every run writes the same bytes.
